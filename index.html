  • Individual & dependent relatives: RM9,000 (already applied automatically below — don't add again)

  • Children below 18: RM2,000 each

Childcare/TASKA, EPF & life insurance, parents' medical, and EV charging aren't listed here since they're already tracked as Ledger categories — photograph those receipts instead of entering them in this field. LHDN updates relief amounts most years — verify before relying on this list.

Backup & restore

Your receipts live only in this browser's storage. Clearing cookies/site data, switching phones, or uninstalling Chrome will erase them — always back up first if you're about to do any of that.

Downloads every receipt and donation across all years, sorted into folders, with a manifest that lets this app restore it exactly.

Only restores zips created by this app's backup button (it needs the manifest.json inside). Records already in your ledger are skipped, so restoring the same backup twice won't duplicate anything.

Data

Everything is stored locally on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded except the receipt image sent to Gemini when you're online and a key is set.
